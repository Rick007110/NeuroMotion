# Code of Conduct

NeuroMotion is built by animators, programmers and physics enthusiasts from all over
the world, with very different levels of experience. We want everyone to feel welcome
here.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source, Rust or
  animation.
- Critique ideas and code, never people. Explain why, and suggest what would be better.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Respect people's time: stay on topic, and search before opening an issue.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on what goes into the project, and that
  "not now" is not "never".

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language, about anyone's
  background, identity, body, ability or experience.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a topic after being asked to stop.
- Pressuring contributors to break the project's
  [clean-room rules](CONTRIBUTING.md#1-clean-room-no-endorphin-code-files-or-assets).

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and in any other space
that represents NeuroMotion, such as chat channels or events.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, contact the maintainers privately: through the
contact details on the GitHub profile of the project owner,
[@Rick007110](https://github.com/Rick007110). Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and warn, temporarily
ban or permanently ban people who break this code. The response fits the behaviour:
usually a private word first, a ban for serious or repeated harm.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
